# My Favorites



##  Favorite Series: The Fate Franchise

**Fate** is a *multimedia franchise* created by Kinoko Nasu and developed by TYPE-MOON. It began with the visual novel **Fate/stay night** in 2004.

> "Masters summon Servants to fight in the Holy Grail War."

### Why I Like It

- Heroic Spirits from history and legend, like King Arthur and Gilgamesh
- Strong world-building and magic system
- Great animation, especially from the studio ufotable
- Memorable characters and emotional storytelling

## Favorite Movie: Ready Player One

**Ready Player One** is a *2018 science fiction adventure film* directed by **Steven Spielberg**. It is based on the novel by Ernest Cline.

### Story Summary

The story is set in the year `2045`, where people escape their harsh lives in a virtual reality world called the **OASIS**. Wade Watts, a teenager, joins a contest to find a hidden Easter egg left by the OASIS creator, James Halliday, and win control of the OASIS.

### What I Love About It

- [x] Lots of pop culture references
- [x] Exciting action scenes
- [x] Amazing visual effects
